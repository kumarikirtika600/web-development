my experience with books

When I was younger, I didn’t notice how much I needed books. At first, I picked them up out of curiosity, but soon they became my quiet friends. I would spend hours lost in their pages, forgetting the world around me. They laughed with me, cried with me, and taught me things I couldn’t learn anywhere else. Over time, reading stopped being just a hobby—it became part of who I am, shaping my thoughts and staying with me through every moment of my life.
